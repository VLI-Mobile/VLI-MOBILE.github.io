01 · THE VITA-LIFE APPROACH

Convenience without compromising on care.

Vita-Life Infusions brings mobile IV infusion services to suitable private locations, giving clients the convenience of care where they are.

Our approach begins before the infusion itself: with an initial screening, appropriate clinical assessment and clear communication about suitability, expectations and aftercare.

Choosing an infusion online is not a clinical recommendation. Your treatment is determined only after appropriate assessment.

06 · FREQUENT QUESTIONS
Do I have to book an infusion after my screening?

No. The initial screening call is complimentary and carries no obligation to proceed.

Can I choose any infusion I want?

You can tell us what you are interested in, but selecting an infusion does not guarantee that it will be clinically appropriate for you. Suitability is assessed individually.

Where are mobile infusions provided?

Service areas and suitable appointment locations will be confirmed with you before booking.

Is the equipment reused?

Vita-Life's service standard is sterile, single-use equipment and supplies for each client and appointment.

What if the nurse decides an infusion isn't appropriate?

Your safety comes first. The registered nurse may recommend an alternative or decline treatment if the infusion is not clinically appropriate.
